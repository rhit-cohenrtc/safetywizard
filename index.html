Sage Cohen-Rider

Sage (they/them) is a theatre artist currently based in Potomac, Maryland, and a recent graduate of Connecticut College. They mainly work in lighting, but also do acting, writing, costumes, props, and stagehand duties. They love being able to build shows as a team and bring creative ideas into reality. Pushing the storytelling and emotions is key. Shows already exist out in the ether, and the fun is in pulling them into our world!
